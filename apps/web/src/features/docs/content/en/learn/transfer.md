# Investigate a transfer made of several requests

**For:** Support, Developer, Analyst.

**Task:** a client says the transfer did not arrive. You have the transfer ID.

1. Paste the transfer ID into the search field and click “Find”. There will be several requests: all of them concern this transfer.

   ![List of requests of one transfer, all with the “Success” status](shot:learn-transfer-results)

2. Open any of them. In the header click “Open operation”.

   ![Request header, with a number marking the “Open operation · Transfer …5c6d” button](shot:learn-transfer-button)

3. Read the outcome and the sentence under it. Here it is “Declined”, although all requests are green. The “per callback” label means that the status came from another system. The link in the sentence leads to the request you need.

   ![Operation header: “Declined”, the “per callback” label, a sentence about what happened](shot:learn-transfer-outcome)

4. Read the table from top to bottom. This is the order of events: who called, when, how long it took and how it ended.

   ![Operation requests table: creation, background processing, callback, partner polls](shot:learn-transfer-table)

5. Click the arrow on the right of the row with the “Declined” outcome. The steps of this request and the “Open request” button appear.

   ![Expanded callback row: a small diagram and “Open request”](shot:learn-transfer-row)

6. Click “Open request”. “To operation” and the request number appear at the top. This way you can move between requests and come back.

   ![Request header: “To operation” and “Request 3 of 3”](shot:learn-transfer-position)

## What you should get

You know how the transfer ended, which request gave this outcome and how the case went over time.

If you see “No other requests found in the window.”, click “Search in a ±12 h window”.
